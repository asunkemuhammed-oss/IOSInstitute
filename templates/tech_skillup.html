Front-end Engineering

Front-end engineering focuses on creating the beautiful face of a website or app. It’s about designing and building the parts that users see and interact with like the page structure, layouts, buttons, images, and more. Front-end engineers use languages like HTML, CSS, React JS, JavaScript, and more.
